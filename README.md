# 3-Statement Financial Analysis & Integrated Forecast — Laurus Labs Ltd

**Company:** Laurus Labs Limited (NSE: LAURUSLABS)
**Role:** Financial Analyst / Investment Banking Financial Modeling
**Team:** 1 | Personal project | 3–4 weeks
**Tools:** Advanced Excel, Python, SQL, Power BI, Tableau, Financial Reporting, Financial Statement Analysis

## Real business problem
Laurus Labs is expanding CDMO, formulations/API and advanced manufacturing capabilities. The analyst question is whether stronger revenue and operating leverage can translate into sustainable cash generation while the company funds capacity expansion and manages working capital and debt.

## Research questions
1. What drove the FY2024–FY2026 change in revenue, EBITDA and PAT?
2. How do CDMO and Affordable Medicines growth affect the operating model?
3. How do inventory, receivables and payables affect cash conversion?
4. Can operating cash flow fund CapEx and shareholder distributions?
5. What happens to liquidity and leverage if growth, margins, working capital or CapEx deviate from plan?

## Model architecture
Historical IS + BS + CF → driver analysis → 5-year integrated forecast → working capital → PP&E/CapEx/D&A → debt/interest → cash → equity → balance-sheet check → scenarios → dashboards → finance memo.

Historical values are sourced/derived from Laurus Labs' FY2025-26 reporting; forecasts are explicitly analyst assumptions.
