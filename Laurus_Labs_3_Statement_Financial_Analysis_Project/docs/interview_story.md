# STAR Interview Story

## Situation
I wanted a real Indian-company project that demonstrated integrated financial modeling rather than only valuation. I chose Laurus Labs because its CDMO and Affordable Medicines businesses are expanding while the company is investing in manufacturing capacity.

## Task
The objective was to understand whether higher growth and operating leverage could translate into sustainable cash generation while managing working capital and CapEx.

## Action
I used Laurus Labs' annual-report and investor-relations disclosures, built historical Income Statement, Balance Sheet and Cash Flow layers, modeled revenue and EBITDA drivers, linked inventory/receivables/payables through operating-day assumptions, and built a five-year integrated forecast. I added Base/Bull/Bear scenarios and reproduced key calculations in Python and SQL.

## Result
The project created an integrated financial-analysis system that shows how business growth, margins, working capital and CapEx affect PAT, CFO, FCF, cash and financial risk. It also gave me a concrete interview story around a real Indian listed company and a genuine finance trade-off: growth investment versus cash conversion.
