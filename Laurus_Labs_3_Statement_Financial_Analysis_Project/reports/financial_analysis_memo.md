# Laurus Labs — Three-Statement Financial Analysis Memo

## Real business problem
Laurus Labs is scaling CDMO and Affordable Medicines while investing in manufacturing and advanced capabilities. The key finance question is whether growth and operating leverage can generate enough cash to fund CapEx and working capital without excessive debt or liquidity pressure.

## Key drivers
Revenue growth, business mix, EBITDA margin, inventory days, receivable days, payable days and CapEx intensity.

## Analyst conclusion framework
Evaluate revenue quality, margin expansion, cash conversion, working-capital intensity, CapEx requirements and debt/cash trajectory under Base/Bull/Bear scenarios.

## Important model-control rule
The detailed FY2026 balance sheet must be reconciled to the full annual report before the model is presented as fully professional. This package deliberately labels incomplete historical lines so the analyst can finish the reconciliation rather than invent figures.
